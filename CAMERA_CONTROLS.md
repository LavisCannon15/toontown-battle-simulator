# Camera controls

Over the unobstructed battle scene:

- Right mouse drag: rotate around the battle.
- Middle mouse drag: pan the view.
- Mouse wheel: zoom in/out.
- Home: restore the normal battle view.

Controls work during attack playback. Camera movement does not change battle calculations. Zoom and elevation have limits to prevent going underground or inside the focus point. The wheel scrolls the battle log when hovered and the SOS collection while open; other menus block camera input. Button and panel positions remain fixed.
